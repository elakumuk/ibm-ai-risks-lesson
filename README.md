# Five Ways AI Can Get You Fired

A 7-minute, slides-only mini-lesson on the IBM Technology video **"Five AI Risks That Can Get You Fired—And How to Avoid Them"** by Martin Keen, published May 24, 2026. The video is part of the playlist [Martin's IBM Technology Videos](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLr2lYjbHhleF6gZTegJrQ8m_fREBuf3cT).

**Video:** https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1m55T8xST9s
**Live slides:** https://elakumuk.github.io/ibm-ai-risks-lesson/

## Lesson plan (7:00)

| Time | Slide | Point |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00 | 1. Title | Setup: "a little bit clickbait," but each risk has already ended careers |
| 0:20 | 2. Map | The five risks as an escalation, plus a quick poll |
| 1:00 | 3. Shadow AI | Unapproved tools; 1 in 5 orgs breached via shadow AI (IBM 2025) |
| 1:45 | 4. Data leakage | Paste → server → training → gone; why bans fail and governance works |
| 2:40 | 5. Hallucination laundering | Unverified output goes out under your name |
| 3:35 | 6. Prompt injection | Direct vs. indirect (hidden in documents, emails and web pages) |
| 4:35 | 7. Unauthorized agents | Agent capabilities; the zombie-agent backdoor |
| 5:35 | 8. Pattern | Risk → who gets blamed → matching control |
| 6:15 | 9. Takeaways | Monday-morning checklist and the bonus sixth risk |
